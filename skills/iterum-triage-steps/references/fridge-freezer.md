# Fridge-freezer — fault patterns

> **Status: draft, requires engineer validation.** Section keys are now real Iterum taxonomy slugs from Iterum's issue history. The fault content beneath them is still general appliance knowledge, not Iterum service data, and needs engineer sign-off before resident use.

Covers fridge-freezers, larder fridges and freezers, freestanding and integrated. A meaningful share of reported faults on this appliance type are setting, placement or loading issues rather than failures — read every section with that in mind.

## How to use this file

The caller passes a `fault_slug`. Match the section heading exactly. No match means use `_other`.

---

## `fridge_not_cooling` — Not Cooling

| Likely cause | Resident-safe check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature setting changed | Ask what the dial or display reads | Knocked, or reset after a power cut |
| Door not sealing or left ajar | Paper test — see `fridge_door_seal`; ask whether the alarm sounded | Shelves and tall bottles foul the door |
| Vents blocked by food | Ask whether food is packed against the back wall | Restricts circulation |
| Ambient outside the unit's rating | Ask where it is sited — garage, outhouse, unheated utility | Many units have a minimum ambient rating and will not run correctly below it: placement, not a fault |
| Restocked with warm items | Ask what changed in the last 24 hours | Recovery takes time. Not a fault |
| Unit recently moved | Ask when, and how soon it was switched on | Compressor models must stand upright several hours before power-on |
| No power | Check the socket switch and the plug | Never a breaker reset |
| Condenser coils dusty | — | Behind the appliance. Never ask them to move it. Note it |
| Thermostat or sensor failure | — | Engineer |
| Compressor or sealed system failure | Ask whether they hear the compressor | Engineer. See replace-leaning below |

**Engineer-only signals:** compressor running continuously without cooling; no compressor sound at all; ice on rear pipework; any oily residue (refrigerant leak); anything behind the appliance.

## `fridge_over_freezing` — Over-Freezing

| Likely cause | Resident-safe check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Thermostat set too cold | Confirm the setting | Most common by a margin |
| Items against the rear wall | Ask how the shelves are loaded | Coldest point is the back wall |
| Door seal failure, door ajar, or frequent opening | Paper test — see `fridge_door_seal`; ask about usage | Warm humid air drives ice |
| Manual-defrost model due a defrost | Ask whether it is frost-free | Ice is expected there; the resident can defrost it |
| Defrost system failure | — | Frost-free only: ice there is a fault. Engineer |
| Damper or thermostat fault | — | Engineer |

**Engineer-only signals:** ice on rear pipework; grinding from a fan obstructed by ice; any oily residue.

## `fridge_leaking` — Leaking Water

| Likely cause | Resident-safe check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Blocked defrost drain hole | A small hole at the back of the fridge compartment floor — clear gently by hand with a cotton bud; mop with a cloth or towel | Resident-safe. Usual cause of water inside |
| Door seal failure | Paper test — see `fridge_door_seal` | Warm air ingress, then condensation |
| Blocked freezer drain, evaporation tray, or a leak | Note where the water sits | Engineer |

**Engineer-only signals:** water underneath the unit; anything needing the unit moved or a panel removed; oily residue.

## `fridge_noise` — Excessive Noise

| Likely cause | Resident-safe check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Normal operation | Ask them to describe the sound | Gurgling, ticking and occasional clicks are normal refrigerant and thermostat operation. Not a fault — say so factually rather than routing |
| Items on top, or contact with a wall | Ask what is on or beside it | Removing loose items is resident-safe |
| Unit not level | Ask whether it rocks | Levelling needs it moved — engineer |
| Compressor or fan | — | Loud hum or buzz. Engineer |
| Fan obstructed by ice | — | Grinding. Engineer |

**Engineer-only signals:** grinding; loud humming or buzzing; any noise needing the unit moved or opened to locate.

## `fridge_light_fault` — Light Not Working

| Likely cause | Resident-safe check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lamp failed | Confirm the light is out and cooling normal | Never ask a resident to change an internal lamp — a live fitting inside a cavity |
| Door switch failure | Ask whether it stays on, or never comes on | Engineer |

Minor fault. If cooling is unaffected, note it for the next visit rather than dispatching for it alone.

**Engineer-only signals:** lamp, fitting and door switch alike.

## `fridge_door_seal` — Door Seal Issue

| Likely cause | Resident-safe check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Seal dirty or sticky | Wipe the seal and its seating with a cloth | Resident-safe |
| Seal perished, torn or deformed | **Paper test:** close the door on a sheet of paper and pull. Firm resistance means the seal holds. Repeat at several points | Referenced from `fridge_not_cooling` and `fridge_over_freezing` |
| Door obstructed by contents | Ask whether shelves or bottles foul the door | Common, easily fixed |
| Hinge misalignment or dropped door | — | Engineer |

**Engineer-only signals:** hinge or alignment work; seal replacement.

## `fridge_smell` — Smells

| Likely cause | Resident-safe check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Spoiled food | Empty and clean out with a cloth and a bowl of warm soapy water | Most common by far |
| Blocked drain hole | Clear by hand with a cotton bud — see `fridge_leaking` | Stagnant water |
| Seal failure letting warm air in | Paper test — see `fridge_door_seal` | Condensation, then mould |
| Chemical or burning smell | Switch off at the wall socket | Different problem. Route or escalate — not a cleaning issue |

**Engineer-only signals:** any chemical, burning or electrical smell; oily residue; smell persisting after a clean and drain clear.

| Likely cause | Resident-safe check | Notes |

| Displayed code or fault light | Record verbatim; photograph it | Pass through unchanged |

## `fridge_other` — Other

Confirm the symptom in the resident's own words, verbatim. Only these four checks are safe for any fridge fault.

| Likely cause | Resident-safe check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature setting | Ask what the dial or display reads | Frequently knocked |
| Door not closing | Ask whether it shuts fully and whether the alarm sounded | Paper test if unsure |
| Vents blocked | Ask how the shelves are loaded | Restricts circulation |
| Ambient outside rating | Ask where it is sited | Garages and unheated rooms are placement |

Route early rather than improvising.

**Engineer-only signals:** anything the four checks above do not answer.

## Replace-leaning faults

- Compressor failure
- Sealed system fault or refrigerant leak — economically unviable on most domestic units
- Evaporator failure
- Cabinet or liner damage where insulation is compromised

## Typical service life

10–12 years. Longer than laundry appliances, and sealed-system faults are the dominant replacement trigger rather than gradual wear.
